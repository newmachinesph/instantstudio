## How Ocean Material's #PictureTheProblem Campaign Delivered Performance Through Personalisation – Powered by InstantStudio

Ocean pollution is one of the most pressing issues of our time – but for many, it remains invisible. Ocean Material®, a leader in sustainable materials, decided to change that with a campaign designed not just to inform, but to immerse.

With InstantStudio's AI-powered campaign engine, Ocean Material launched #PictureTheProblem, a personalised campaign experience that placed users inside hyper-realistic scenes of environmental devastation. It wasn't abstract. It was immediate. And it worked.

## From Idea to Activation – Overnight

Ocean Material's CEO, Toni Kienberger, posed a bold question:

> "How do we reach people who don't see the crisis right in front of them? Not everyone lives near a plastic-strewn beach or a deforested landscape… What if they weren't just reading about it, but standing right in the middle of it?" — Toni Kienberger, CEO of Ocean Material®

That idea became reality through InstantStudio. In less than 24 hours, the InstantStudio team delivered a hosted campaign site where users could upload a selfie, choose from four scenes (plastic soup, deforestation, melting ice, or waste crisis), and receive a personalised image placing them directly into the environmental scenario. The result was a striking, visual call to action—tailored to each participant, optimised for sharing.

## Campaign Performance

#PictureTheProblem didn't just generate images—it generated results.

- Thousands of personalised images generated and shared across social media
- High engagement rates driven by emotional, visual storytelling
- Significant organic reach through user-generated sharing

This was a brand message transformed into thousands of personal stories—each one shared by real users, each one extending the campaign's reach.

> "This campaign was a small nudge to help people see the bigger picture. We are the first generation to truly see the damage—and probably the last with a real chance to turn the story around." — Toni Kienberger, CEO of Ocean Material®
